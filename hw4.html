Homework #4 – Photomontage

For this project, I created two photomontages, each composed of 7–8 layered images blended with textures, including one photo of a jellyfish! The first piece focuses on the toxic food culture in America, while the second focuses on war, portraying the aftermath of conflict. I used masking and blending techniques on both montages to merge images seamlessly to create something not only politically charged but also peronally.
