This photo was chosen because... the message on the cup: “It’s our pleasure to serve you” struck me as both ironic and unsettling. In a world where many people work long hours for minimum wage, struggle to afford basic healthcare, and watch essential benefits get stripped away, phrases like this can feel hollow. It reminded me how easily corporate politeness can mask deeper systemic issues and the modern-day grind that so many of us are stuck in just to survive. (Funny enough, the cup was empty, and the student I asked to borrow it from said no three times before finally agreeing — with a deeply unamused look on her face. Honestly, that made the photo even more real.)
